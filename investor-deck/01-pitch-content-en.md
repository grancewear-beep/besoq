**Headline:** The market is split into four options. Each one has a clear weakness.

**Content (two columns):**

**LC Waikiki, DeFacto, Koton**
Affordable, but zero design. Everyone wears the same thing. The 22–32 demographic has outgrown these brands.

**Zara, Pull & Bear, Bershka**
Good design, but expensive. New trends every month — impossible to build a wardrobe.

**Right column:**

**Trendyol & Turkish brands**
Decent value, but 7–14 day delivery. Returns are painful.

**Local Instagram brands**
Small teams. No scale, no speed, weak branding.

**Bottom line (bold):**

There is a gap in the market: smart design + fair price + local production + fast delivery. Nobody fills it.

## Slide 08 — Why BESOQ Wins

**Headline:** Five advantages no competitor has.

**1. Speed**
Order today → package tomorrow. No other Azerbaijani brand can say this.

**2. Local production partner**
Large facility in Baku. All print technologies (embroidery, DTF, DTG, vinyl). 50+ units/day capacity. 3+ year working relationship.

**3. Print-on-demand model**
No warehouse. No dead stock. Order comes in → gets produced → ships out. This reduces capital requirements by 10×.

**4. Brand positioning**
"Be So Quiet" — minimal branding, quiet design. An empty space on the Baku market map that nobody occupies.

## Notes for the presenter

### Before the meeting
- Replace every `[PLACEHOLDER]` with real data
- Prepare 2-minute talking points for each slide (total: ~25 min)
- Anticipate questions:
  - "Why don't your production partners do this themselves?"
  - "How is this different from TOBODY?"
  - "How will you spend the marketing budget?"
  - "What happens if a competitor enters?"
- Have answers ready

### During the meeting
- Don't read the slides — talk to the person
- If you don't know an answer: "I'll check and get back to you" is fine
- At the end, make a clear ask: "Can I have your answer within a week?"

### After the meeting (within 24 hours)
- Send a thank-you email
- Attach written answers to any questions
- If they ask for more detail — send a full business plan (separate document)

### Physical materials to bring
- A real BESOQ t-shirt and hoodie (samples)
- A printed one-page summary (brand overview)
- A business card with your contact info

## Financial disclaimer

All numbers in this deck are projections based on market research and estimated production costs. They are not guarantees. Before presenting to any investor, verify:

1. Your production partner's actual costs (confirm the 12/18/18 ₼ figures)
2. Realistic monthly order growth for your market
3. Actual delivery costs in your city
4. Legal structure of the investment offer (consult a lawyer)

Presenting inaccurate numbers to an investor is the fastest way to lose credibility permanently.
