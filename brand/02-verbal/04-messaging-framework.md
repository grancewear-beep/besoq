# Messaging framework

The messaging framework translates the brand's strategy into specific messages, mapped to specific audiences, surfaces, and moments. It is the bridge between *what the brand stands for* and *what the brand says today*.

---

## Message hierarchy

```
       BRAND PROMISE
             │
             ▼
       PILLAR MESSAGES (4)
             │
             ▼
       PROOF POINTS (per pillar)
             │
             ▼
       SURFACE-SPECIFIC EXPRESSIONS
```

---

## Brand promise (one line)

> a wardrobe that announces nothing and lasts everything.

This is the highest level of the messaging system. It is rarely used verbatim — it is the source from which other messages are derived.

---

## The four pillar messages

Each of the four brand pillars has one canonical message, used as the headline expression of that pillar across all surfaces.

### Pillar — Quiet
> **the loudest thing in the room is the person who has nothing to say.**

### Pillar — Considered
> **every garment has a mill, a weight, and a reason.**

### Pillar — Lasting
> **we will repair this. for as long as you wear it.**

### Pillar — Honest
> **we publish what we got wrong.**

These four lines, taken together, are the verbal identity of the brand. Any new piece of copy should be traceable to at least one of them.

---

## Proof points (the things we point to)

Pillar messages are abstract. Proof points are concrete. Each pillar has at least three.

### Quiet — proof points
- Wordmark size: never larger than 8mm on outerwear.
- Two collections per year — no resort, pre-fall, or capsules.
- No flash sales. No countdown timers. No urgency mechanics.

### Considered — proof points
- Mill name disclosed on every product page.
- Fabric weight in GSM disclosed on every product page.
- Pattern blocks versioned and protected; revisions logged.
- Care instructions in plain English, not symbols alone.

### Lasting — proof points
- Lifetime repair service for every garment.
- Permanent line restocked, not redesigned.
- Spare buttons sewn into the lining of every jacket.
- Materials selected for graceful ageing, not preserved newness.

### Honest — proof points
- Public "what we got wrong" page in the help centre.
- Country of make stated on every product page, even when not flattering.
- No "premium," "luxury," or "exclusive" without a defensible qualifier.
- Price breakdown available on request (cost-of-cloth, cost-of-make, margin).

## Audience-specific framings

The same pillar message is reframed to suit each persona's posture.

### For The Returner (Persona 01)
**Headline message:** "stop chasing. start choosing."
**Why:** she has already left the loud market; we welcome her without lecturing her.
**Surfaces:** brand campaigns, journal, organic social.

### For The Builder (Persona 02)
**Headline message:** "a wardrobe is a system, not a haul."
**Why:** he is wardrobe-building deliberately; we help him with information, not inspiration.
**Surfaces:** PDPs, fabric stories, repeat-customer email flow.

### For The Quiet Professional (Persona 03)
**Headline message:** "modern tailoring, without the noise."
**Why:** she expects rigour; we lead with cut and material credibility.
**Surfaces:** atelier launch, lookbook, wholesale presentations.

## Moment-specific messages

The brand also has reusable messages for predictable moments in the year.

| Moment | Message | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Black Friday / Cyber Monday** | "we are not on sale today. we will not be tomorrow." | Restates anti-discount stance without lecturing. |
| **End-of-season** | "the archive is open. one price. until it is gone." | Defines our version of a "sale" — full price, finite. |
| **New collection launch** | "[collection name]. on site now." | Single line. No exclamation. No countdown. |
| **Restock of permanent line** | "back, in the same fit." | Communicates consistency, which is the permanent line's value. |
| **Repair service reminder** | "we will repair this. for as long as you wear it." | Reused verbatim from the Lasting pillar. |
| **Care reminder (off-season)** | "store it folded. let it rest." | Treats the garment as something the customer is in relationship with. |

## Internal messaging — for the team and for partners

For the team, the message is simpler:
> **shut up and make the garment well.**

For wholesale partners and press, the framing is:
> **independent, contemporary, considered. priced where the post-logo customer is ready to spend.**

---

## How to use this framework

1. Identify the **surface** (PDP, email, hangtag, etc.).
2. Identify the **persona** most likely to encounter that surface.
3. Identify the **pillar** most relevant to the message you need to convey.
4. Choose between the **pillar message**, a **proof point**, or a **persona-framed expression**.
5. Apply the **voice rules** from `02-tone-of-voice.md` to the final copy.

If a piece of copy cannot be traced backward through this framework to a pillar, it should not ship.
