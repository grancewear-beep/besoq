# Glossary & vocabulary controls

A working dictionary of words BESOQ uses, words BESOQ avoids, and words BESOQ has redefined for internal use. This is a living document — additions are made via PR.

---

## House vocabulary (preferred)

These are the words we reach for first.

| BESOQ word | Meaning / nuance |
|---|---|
| **garment** | the preferred word for any single piece of clothing. |
| **cloth** | the preferred word for fabric. ("a 140 gsm cotton cloth.") |
| **wardrobe** | a system of garments owned by a person; not a marketing word. |
| **piece** | acceptable when "garment" would be repetitive. |
| **mill** | the source of the cloth, by name and place. |
| **maker** | the factory or atelier where the garment is sewn. |
| **make** | the process of sewing. ("made in portugal." — never "manufactured.") |
| **weight** | the GSM of a fabric; always disclosed. |
| **hand** | the tactile feel of a fabric. |
| **cut** | the shape of a garment, owned by the brand. |
| **shoulder** | a structural element we discuss often. |
| **proportion** | the relationship between cuts; an identity vector. |
| **silhouette** | the overall outline of a garment on the body. |
| **archive** | past seasonal stock, full price. |
| **permanent** | the always-on core line. |
| **studies** | numbered limited explorations. |
| **release** | when a collection becomes available. (We do not "drop.") |
| **return** | when a permanent piece is restocked. (We do not "re-launch.") |
| **care** | our customer-service function. |
| **repair** | our post-purchase service; an active practice. |
| **season** | spring or autumn — never "fall," never "FW," never "SS." |
| **the garment** | a stand-in for a specific piece, used in editorial copy. |

## Forbidden words & phrases

These are not used in any customer-facing copy. Many are not used internally either.

### The hype family
- iconic
- must-have
- game-changing
- revolutionary
- unprecedented
- next-level
- ultimate
- perfect
- amazing
- incredible
- stunning
- gorgeous
- obsessed
- absolute fire
- 🔥 (and any emoji)

### The urgency family
- limited time
- act fast
- hurry
- last chance
- selling out
- only X left
- before it's gone
- exclusive offer

### The marketing-soft family
- curated
- thoughtfully made
- handcrafted (used loosely)
- artisanal (used loosely)
- bespoke (used loosely)
- premium
- luxury (without qualifier)
- elevated
- elevated essentials

### The corporate-cushion family
- we wanted to let you know that
- as you know
- valued customer
- as a token of our appreciation
- we sincerely hope
- please be advised
- kindly note

### The trade-jargon family
- drop
- capsule (used to mean "small collection we want to sell faster")
- collab (we say "with [partner]")
- IRL
- DM us
- tap link in bio
- swipe up

### Spelling & formatting forbidden
- "F/W," "S/S," "AW," "SS25" — we write "autumn 26," "spring 26."
- "TM," "®" in body copy.
- ALL CAPS in body copy.
- Excessive ellipses... like this...
- Multiple punctuation: "great!!", "really??", "now!!!"

## Redefined terms (for internal clarity)

When we use these words, we mean something specific.

| Word | Common meaning | What we mean |
|---|---|---|
| **collection** | a seasonal release, often >50 SKUs | a release of ≤25 garments, twice a year |
| **launch** | a marketing event | the moment a collection becomes available; not a moment we celebrate |
| **drop** | a small, hyped release | not used; we say "release" or "return" |
| **luxury** | an industry tier | a word we avoid unless we can defend it materially |
| **basics** | undesigned defaults | a category we do not make; our permanent line is *designed*, not basic |
| **minimalist** | an aesthetic | a description we accept but did not choose; we are *quiet*, which is broader |
| **timeless** | a marketing claim | a word we avoid; we say "designed to outlast its season" |
| **investment piece** | a marketing claim | a word we avoid; we say "designed to be re-bought" |

## Loanwords from other languages

When the right word does not exist in English, we use the original word, set in italic.

- *ma* (間, Japanese) — the considered space between things. Used in design notes for layout.
- *wabi-sabi* — used internally for material ageing; rarely public-facing.
- *biellese* — adjective for cloth from Biella; preferred to "Italian wool."

We do not invent loanword aesthetics. We use the word where it is genuinely the better word.

## When in doubt

If a word is not in this glossary and feels uncertain, the test is:

1. Would a luxury house with a quiet posture use it? If yes, ship.
2. Would it appear in a fast-fashion sale email? If yes, do not ship.
3. Does it add information, or only adjective? If only adjective, cut.

The rule of the glossary is the rule of the brand: when in doubt, fewer words.
