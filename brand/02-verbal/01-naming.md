# Naming rationale

## The name

**BESOQ** — pronounced /bɪˈsɒk/ (be-SOCK), in five letters with the stress on the second syllable.

It is an acronym of the brand's founding sentence:

> **Be So Quiet.**

The name is simultaneously the brand's identity, its mission, and its instruction to itself.

---

## Why it works

### 1. It is a sentence
Most clothing brands are named after a person, a place, or an invented word. BESOQ is named after a behaviour. This is rare and defensible — the name cannot be separated from the brand's philosophy.

### 2. It is short
Five letters. One syllable in casual speech (people will compress it to "be-sock"). Easy to type, easy to find, easy to remember.

### 3. It is unique in the trademark space
"BESOQ" produces no meaningful collisions in fashion or apparel registries (verified before adoption). This is unusual for a five-letter mark in the post-2020 brand-saturation era.

### 4. It hides a story
The acronym is not announced loudly. New customers see "BESOQ" and read it as a word; they discover the meaning later, often through the garment, the hangtag, or a quiet line of editorial copy. This **delayed reveal** is a brand asset.

### 5. It enforces the brand
A brand named "Be So Quiet" cannot be loud without becoming a hypocrite. The name is a permanent internal check.

## Pronunciation

| Region | Accepted pronunciation |
|---|---|
| English-speaking markets | /bɪˈsɒk/ — "be-SOCK" |
| Azerbaijani / Turkic markets | /beˈsoq/ — "be-SOQ", with the final Q as in *qardaş* |
| French / Italian markets | "be-SOK" with a soft K |

All three are acceptable. The brand does not correct pronunciations — quiet brands do not police their own name.

## Casing rules

| Form | Use |
|---|---|
| `besoq` | **Primary.** All product, packaging, web, social, marketing copy. |
| `BESOQ` | **Editorial only.** Press releases, legal documents, retail signage, photography credits. |
| `Besoq` | **Forbidden.** Title case is not used in any context. |
| `BeSoQ` | **Forbidden.** The acronym is internal; the surface form is one word. |

---

## How to write it in a sentence

✅ "i bought a coat from besoq last winter."
✅ "BESOQ — Spring 2026."
❌ "i bought a coat from Besoq last winter."
❌ "i bought a coat from BeSoQ last winter."
❌ "i bought a coat from BESOQ™ last winter." *(the trademark mark is reserved for legal documents only)*

## Domain & handle system

| Surface | Identifier |
|---|---|
| Primary domain | `besoq.com` |
| Email domain | `besoq.com` |
| Social handles | `@besoq` (universal across platforms; never `@besoq_official`, `@theofficialbesoq`, etc.) |
| Press inquiries | `press@besoq.com` |
| Customer care | `care@besoq.com` (not "support" — we care for, we do not support) |
| Repair service | `repair@besoq.com` |

If the primary handle is unavailable on a platform, the brand will not register a workaround handle. It will simply not be on that platform until the handle is acquired.

---

## What the name will never be used for

- ❌ A hashtag campaign (#BESOQwinter, #wearBESOQ — both forbidden).
- ❌ A jingle, audio logo, or sonic mnemonic.
- ❌ A founder-led personal brand ("BESOQ by [Name]" — never).
- ❌ A licensed extension (BESOQ-branded fragrance, BESOQ-branded eyewear — never).
- ❌ A secondary line ("BESOQ Studio," "BESOQ Lab," "BESOQ Black" — see `brand/01-strategy/07-brand-architecture.md`).

---

## Acronym integrity

The expansion is fixed. It is, and will always be:

> **B**e **S**o **Q**uiet.

It is not "Beautifully Sourced, Original Quality." It is not "Better Style of Quiet." It is not anything else. The acronym is the philosophy; tampering with it tampers with the brand.

The expansion is used sparingly in marketing — once per surface, at most. It is most powerful when discovered, not declared.
