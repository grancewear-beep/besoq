# Tagline system

BESOQ uses a layered tagline system, not a single line. The system is hierarchical: each layer has a specific role and a specific application surface. They reinforce each other; they do not substitute for each other.

---

## The five tiers

### Tier 1 — Master tagline
> **be so quiet.**

The acronym, written out. This is the foundational line. It appears on packaging, on the first screen of the website, on the inside of every garment label, and on the back of every business card.

**Used:** sparingly. No more than once per surface.
**Never:** translated. Never set in uppercase except in the press-release exception.

---

### Tier 2 — Editorial line
> **whisper, don't shout.**

The campaign-level line. Used in lookbook covers, hero campaigns, and seasonal launches. More poetic than Tier 1, but still rooted in the same idea.

**Used:** once per campaign, in a single hero placement.
**Never:** combined with Tier 1 on the same surface.

---

### Tier 3 — Functional lines
Three permitted variations, used to support specific contexts. None of them is the tagline; they are statements of philosophy.

| Line | Context |
|---|---|
| **cloth before logo.** | Material-led campaigns, fabric stories, sourcing pages. |
| **less is loud enough.** | Customer-facing, anti-discount, anti-hype messaging. |
| **the garment is the message.** | Editorial pieces, journal entries, brand-led content. |

**Used:** one per surface, only when the surface's context matches the line.
**Never:** stacked together. Never used as page titles. Never reused across the same campaign.

---

### Tier 4 — Sign-off
> **— besoq**

The em-dash plus lowercase wordmark. Appears at the end of every email, every social post that requires a sign-off, and every owned editorial piece.

**Used:** as an end-mark only. Always with the en-space before the dash.
**Never:** with a period after `besoq` in this context. The sign-off is the period.

---

### Tier 5 — Internal motto
> **shut up and make the garment well.**

Internal-only. Never appears on any customer-facing surface. It is what we say to ourselves before we ship anything. It is, in a sense, our most honest tagline — and that is precisely why it must remain private. The customer-facing taglines describe the result; this one describes the discipline.

**Used:** team email signatures (optional), internal documents, the studio wall.
**Never:** publicly.

## Forbidden tagline behaviours

- ❌ A tagline must never be translated. The brand's English-language voice is universal.
- ❌ A tagline must never be set in italic.
- ❌ A tagline must never be used as a hashtag.
- ❌ A tagline must never be paired with another tagline on the same surface.
- ❌ A tagline must never be modified for a campaign ("be so quiet — for spring" — never).
- ❌ A tagline must never be used as a button label, CTA, or link text.

---

## A note on quietness in the system

Note that no tagline is more than five words. Note that none of them is a question. Note that none of them rhymes. Note that none of them is clever for its own sake. This is intentional. A clever tagline is loud. A precise tagline is quiet.
