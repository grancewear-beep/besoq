# Retail & store

When BESOQ has a physical space, the space is treated as a garment: composed, calm, recognisable without a label. The retail philosophy mirrors the brand philosophy in three dimensions.

This document specifies the brand's standards for any owned retail location, pop-up, atelier, or wholesale showroom.

---

## Philosophy

> the store is a quiet room with garments in it.

A BESOQ store is closer to a gallery than to a shop. The customer should feel the space slow them down — a posture-corrector at the threshold.

---

## 1. The threshold

The first three metres of the store define the experience. They are the most important architectural decision in the system.

| Element | Spec |
|---|---|
| **Door material** | Solid wood, painted bone, or natural oak. Never glass. |
| **Door handle** | Brushed brass or steel. Round or rectangular. No branded handles. |
| **Threshold mat** | None. Just the floor. |
| **Welcome scent** | None. The space is unscented. |
| **Welcome music** | None. Silence, or low ambient (ECM-label or Hiroshi Yoshimura, library only). |
| **Welcome greeting** | "welcome." Not "hello, welcome to BESOQ, can I help you?" |

---

## 2. The architecture

### Walls
- Bone, lime-washed plaster — never paint.
- Texture visible at close range, smooth at distance.
- No mounted artwork, no posters, no campaign images on walls. The garments are the art.

### Floor
- Either:
  - Wide-plank natural oak (untreated or hard-waxed), **or**
  - Polished concrete in a warm bone tint.
- No carpet, no rugs, no tiles.

### Ceiling
- Painted in the same bone as the walls. Never white.
- Exposed structural elements (beams, ducts) are acceptable if they read calm; otherwise covered.
- Lighting is recessed or surface-mounted in matte black; no decorative pendants except in the fitting rooms (see below).

### Furniture
- Solid timber or steel, in bone or ash finishes only.
- One bench in the centre, made of a single slab.
- One brass or steel rail per garment category along the perimeter.
- A single plinth at the front of the store, displaying one garment from the current collection.
- Nothing more.

---

## 3. Display

### Garment density
A BESOQ store is **75% empty space** by area. Density rules:

| Surface area | Maximum garments |
|---|---|
| 50m² store | ≤ 35 garments displayed |
| 100m² store | ≤ 60 garments displayed |
| 200m² store | ≤ 100 garments displayed |

These limits are enforced. A store that exceeds them is a violation.

### Hanging
- All garments on bone or natural-oak hangers — no plastic, no wire, no padded.
- 12cm minimum spacing between hangers (most stores keep 16–20cm).
- Garments hung **with the closure facing the customer** (button placket forward, zip forward).
- Sleeves never tied, never folded across the body.

### Folding
- Knitwear is folded on a single shelf, four pieces per stack maximum, with a 4cm gap between stacks.
- Folded garments are aligned to a precision the customer notices subliminally.

### Plinths
- One plinth per store, at the front.
- One garment on the plinth, on a bust or hanger.
- Rotated weekly during a collection.

### Forbidden displays
- ❌ Mannequins with full styling and accessories.
- ❌ Mood boards or trend boards on the wall.
- ❌ Stacked tables piled high with knitwear.
- ❌ Window displays with text or graphics.
- ❌ "New Arrival" signs, "Sale" signs, "Last One" stickers.
- ❌ Any printed material in the store (no flyers, no lookbooks on the bench, no postcards).

---

## 4. Window

The window is the most public surface of the store. Treat it as the brand's most visible page.

### Two acceptable window treatments

**Window A — single garment.**
- One garment hung on a single rail, centred, against the bone-plastered back wall.
- Soft daylight or a single overhead spot.
- No props, no signage, no graphics.

**Window B — empty.**
- A completely empty window, lit with a single overhead spot warming the bone-plastered back wall.
- Used for the week before a collection launch, and the week after a collection's closing.
- An empty window is the brand's most confident statement.

### Forbidden window treatments
- ❌ Multiple mannequins.
- ❌ Vinyl signage on the glass.
- ❌ Seasonal campaign images.
- ❌ Discount messaging.
- ❌ "Coming soon" countdowns.
- ❌ Holiday décor of any kind.

---

## 5. Lighting

### Colour temperature
**3500K** — warm white, slightly cooler than candlelight, slightly warmer than daylight. The same temperature across the entire store. Never mix colour temperatures.

### Direction
- Garments lit **from above and slightly behind**, allowing the customer to see drape and shadow without glare.
- The customer is never lit harder than the garment.
- Mirrors lit from above with a 3500K diffuse source so the customer sees themselves accurately.

### Brightness
- 250–400 lux at garment height.
- Threshold area slightly dimmer (200 lux) — a subtle "you have entered" signal.

### Forbidden lighting
- ❌ Coloured lighting.
- ❌ Strobing or "atmospheric" lights.
- ❌ Track lighting that points at customers.
- ❌ Mirrored fixtures, chrome reflectors, gilded chandeliers.

---

## 6. Fitting room

The fitting room is the most considered surface in the store — the moment the customer becomes a customer.

| Element | Spec |
|---|---|
| **Size** | 1.6 × 1.6m minimum |
| **Door** | Solid wood door, full height, with a brass slide bolt — never a curtain |
| **Walls** | Bone plaster |
| **Mirror** | Full-height, slightly off-vertical (1°) for accurate body view |
| **Lighting** | One soft overhead at 3500K + one indirect wash from below |
| **Bench** | Single timber bench, 600 × 350 × 450mm |
| **Hooks** | Three brass hooks at staggered heights |
| **Floor** | Same as the rest of the store |
| **Sound** | Same as the rest of the store — no music in the fitting room |
| **Camera** | Absolutely none. No security cameras face the fitting room. The dignity of the room is non-negotiable. |

### What is provided in every fitting room
- A clean cotton mat for shoes-off use, replaced daily.
- A small bone tray for personal items.
- A glass of water on a small bone-glazed coaster, refreshed when the room is cleaned between customers.

### What is forbidden
- ❌ Branded slippers, robes, or "fit kit" items.
- ❌ Background music piped into the fitting room.
- ❌ "Need help?" intercom buttons.
- ❌ Lighting that flatters falsely. The customer should see themselves accurately.

---

## 7. The bench

A single, central bench is the brand's seat. It is the only piece of furniture customers sit on, and it must be **comfortable** and **unfussy**.

| Spec | Value |
|---|---|
| Material | Solid timber (oak, ash, or walnut) |
| Length | 1.8–2.4m |
| Construction | Joinery, no visible fasteners |
| Cushioning | None — the timber is the seat |
| Upholstery | None |

The bench accepts the customer who wants to slow down. There is no stack of magazines on it, no curated coffee-table books — the bench is a piece of architecture, not a touchpoint.

---

## 8. Staff

The team — at the door, on the floor, at the till — is the brand made human.

### Conduct rules
1. **Greet quietly.** "welcome." That is enough.
2. **Approach only when asked.** Never offer help unprompted.
3. **Never compliment a customer's existing clothes.** It is a sales technique that the brand does not use.
4. **Never hover.** Stand still or sit at the till until invited.
5. **Never use hype words.** Never describe a garment as "stunning," "amazing," "iconic," "perfect for you."
6. **Speak in materials.** Cloth weight, mill, country of make — these are what staff are trained to know.
7. **Wear the brand.** Staff wear current-season BESOQ during all open hours. They do not, however, look like uniformed staff. They look like customers who happen to work there.

### What staff never do
- ❌ Wear name badges.
- ❌ Wear earpieces.
- ❌ Hand the customer a tablet to "sign up for our newsletter."
- ❌ Ask the customer to leave a review at the door.
- ❌ Photograph the customer (for the wall, for social, for any purpose).
- ❌ Use first-person plural sales language ("we love this on you").

---

## 9. Music

The default state of the store is **silence**. When music is played, it is:

- **Library:** ECM Records ambient catalogue, Hiroshi Yoshimura, Nils Frahm (instrumental), Ryuichi Sakamoto (instrumental).
- **Volume:** below conversation level. The music must never be the loudest thing in the room — that role is reserved, by definition, for nothing.
- **Selection:** a curated 6-hour silent loop, refreshed quarterly.

The store has no playlist on Spotify. There is no QR code that lets you "listen to what's playing in store." The music is part of the architecture, not a marketing artefact.

---

## 10. Scent

The brand position on scent is unequivocal:

> **the store is unscented.**

No diffusers. No signature scent. No candles burning. The smell of the store is the smell of bone-plastered walls, oak, cotton, wool, and the people inside it.

This is a deliberate choice. Most luxury retail uses heavy proprietary scent as a memory hook. BESOQ refuses on the basis of Pillar 1 (Quiet) — scent is loud by definition.

---

## 11. Receipt & exit

The customer's exit must be as quiet as the threshold.

### Receipt
- Printed on a small bone-coloured slip.
- Single-colour ash type.
- Wordmark at the top, item details in the middle, total at the bottom.
- A single line at the foot: `— besoq`.
- No QR code, no "scan to leave a review," no "earn points."

### Goodbye
- Staff: "thank you."
- Door: opened by the customer, not held aggressively.
- No "have a great day," no "see you soon," no "tag us when you wear it."

---

## 12. Pop-up & wholesale showrooms

When BESOQ shows in a pop-up or wholesale showroom (where full architectural control is impossible), the brand's adaptation rules:

1. Bring **bone-toned cotton sheeting** to drape over any walls that are not already bone. Do not paint.
2. Use **brass or steel hangers** brought from the studio; do not accept the venue's plastic hangers.
3. Bring **a single bench** if the venue does not provide one.
4. Display **fewer garments than the room could hold** — the brand's rule of 75% empty space is portable.
5. Replace the venue's lighting only if it is harshly cool. Bring tungsten gels if needed.
6. Play **silence** — even if the venue offers a sound system.

A pop-up that cannot meet these standards is, by default, not a BESOQ pop-up. Refuse the venue.

---

## 13. The empty test

The single most important retail test:

> **photograph the store with no customers and no staff. is it boring?**

If yes — too austere. Add a single, considered detail.
If no — calm, considered, alive. The store is correct.
If the answer is "loud" — the brand has drifted. Pull back.

A BESOQ store, photographed empty, should look like a room someone has just left.
