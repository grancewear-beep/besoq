# Lookbook

The lookbook is the brand's only printed marketing artefact. It is not a catalogue. It is not a sales tool. It is a small, considered book, produced once per season, designed to outlast the season it documents.

---

## Lookbook philosophy

> the lookbook is the season, made permanent.

A BESOQ lookbook should be sittable on a coffee table without explanation, two years after the season has ended, alongside a Zumthor monograph or an ECM box set. If it does not survive that test, it is not a BESOQ lookbook.

---

## 1. Cadence

Two lookbooks per year, one per seasonal collection:

- **Spring lookbook** — published with the spring collection in March.
- **Autumn lookbook** — published with the autumn collection in September.

The permanent line is not lookbooked. It is documented on the website only. This is intentional — permanent pieces don't need a season's frame.

## 2. Format

| Spec | Value |
|---|---|
| **Trim size** | 200 × 280mm (slightly narrower than A4) |
| **Page count** | 64 pages, including cover |
| **Binding** | Section-sewn, exposed thread spine, no glue |
| **Cover stock** | Old Mill Premium White Linen, 250 gsm |
| **Interior stock** | Munken Pure, 100 gsm |
| **Print method** | Four-colour offset (image pages) + single-colour offset (text pages) |
| **Print run** | 1,000 copies per season — never reprinted |

### Cover construction

A bone-toned linen-finish cover, debossed with:
- The wordmark `besoq` at the top, 32mm wide, 24mm from the top edge.
- The line name `— autumn 26` immediately below.
- Nothing else. No image on the cover.

The cover is intentionally **near-blank** — a paper object the reader has to open to enter.

## 3. Page architecture

The interior follows a strict structure of 64 pages.

```
01     ─ blank
02     ─ blank
03     ─ blank
04     ─ blank
05     ─ colophon (top), table of contents (bottom)
06     ─ blank
07     ─ opening line (single line of editorial type)
08     ─ blank
09–10  ─ first garment plate (right) + spec page (left)
...
[20 garment spreads = 40 pages]
...
51     ─ blank
52     ─ material story (text only)
53     ─ material macro photograph
54     ─ blank
55     ─ atelier note (single paragraph)
56     ─ blank
57     ─ end-line (single line of type)
58     ─ blank
59     ─ blank
60     ─ blank
61     ─ blank
62     ─ blank
63     ─ blank
64     ─ wordmark + tagline (back-inside cover)
```

**The math:** of 64 pages, fewer than 30 carry imagery. The rest are deliberate empty pages, blank verso pages opposite plates, and a single material-story spread. The book is, by design, **half white space**.

---

## 4. Plate construction

A "plate" is a single garment image. Every plate follows the same construction.

### Right page — the image
- Full-bleed photograph, edge-to-edge.
- Mode: studio garment, on-body neutral, or architectural (see `03-visual/06-photography.md`).
- One garment per plate. Multiple garments in the same shot are forbidden.

### Left page — the spec

```
the long coat
besoq — autumn 26

cloth     380 gsm wool, 100%
mill      [mill name], biella
make      portugal

[brief paragraph about the garment, 30–60 words,
 set in Times Now Regular at 11pt]

— 380 gsm wool, 100%
— horn buttons
— shoulder built without padding
— hand-finished cuff
```

The spec page is set in two columns:
- Left column (60%): garment name, line, brief paragraph.
- Right column (40%): cloth / mill / make + bullet specs.

Vertical rhythm: 4mm baseline grid throughout.

## 5. Typography

The lookbook uses a slightly different typographic register than the website — more editorial, more serif-led.

| Use | Face | Size |
|---|---|---|
| Garment name (spec page) | Söhne Buch | 22pt |
| Line identifier | Söhne Buch | 11pt |
| Spec table | Söhne Mono | 9pt |
| Editorial paragraph | Times Now Regular | 11pt / 18pt leading |
| Opening / closing lines | Times Now Light | 36pt |
| Material story | Times Now Regular | 13pt / 22pt leading |
| Atelier note | Times Now Italic (used here only) | 11pt / 18pt leading |
| Page number | Söhne Mono | 7pt, bottom-left |
| Folio | Söhne Buch | 7pt, bottom-right (line + page) |

The atelier note on page 55 is the only place in the entire brand system where italic Times Now is used. It is a private signature — a paragraph from the studio, set apart by typography.

---

## 6. Photography in the lookbook

The lookbook is the brand's most photography-intensive surface. The photography rules from `03-visual/06-photography.md` apply, with three additions for print:

1. **Print-graded files only.** Web-graded JPEGs may not be repurposed for the lookbook.
2. **CMYK conversion is final.** Each plate is colour-proofed against the bone-toned Munken Pure paper, not against an RGB display.
3. **No image is reused across two consecutive lookbooks.** Even if a permanent garment appears in both seasons.

---

## 7. Editorial copy

The lookbook contains very few words. The total word count is approximately:

| Surface | Word count |
|---|---|
| Opening line (page 7) | 3–8 words |
| 20 spec pages × 60 words | ≤ 1,200 words |
| Material story (page 52) | 200–300 words |
| Atelier note (page 55) | 80–120 words |
| Closing line (page 57) | 3–8 words |
| **Total** | **≤ 1,700 words** |

A book of 64 pages, with under 1,700 words. That is the brand's editorial discipline.

### Opening lines (across seasons)

- Spring 26: `the cloth wakes.`
- Autumn 26: `eleven months of fittings.`
- Spring 27: `lighter, on purpose.`

Each opening line is a single sentence — never an introduction, never a "letter from the founder." The book begins; the reader enters.

### Closing lines (always the same)

```
be so quiet.

— besoq
```

That is every closing page, every season.

---

## 8. Distribution

The lookbook is **not a free promotional item**. The brand's distribution is:

- **Press**: 80 copies, hand-delivered or sent in a kraft mailer.
- **Wholesale partners**: 60 copies, distributed via showroom appointments.
- **Selected long-term customers**: 200 copies, sent unsolicited to known names.
- **In-store**: 100 copies, available to read in retail locations (one is permanently chained-but-unbound to the bench, in homage to libraries).
- **Sold via the website**: 400 copies, at cost (or a small premium), in `/the-wardrobe` as the only "non-garment" SKU.
- **Studio archive**: 160 copies, kept against future request.

**Total:** 1,000 copies. When they are gone, they are gone. The lookbook is not reprinted.

---

## 9. Digital lookbook

The lookbook is **not** published as a PDF or as an interactive flip-book. The brand does not produce a digital equivalent.

Instead, the seasonal collection page on `besoq.com` is itself the digital expression of the season — a long, scrolling editorial page with the same plates, in the same order, in the same restraint. The print lookbook and the website's seasonal page are companions, not duplicates.

---

## 10. Forbidden lookbook behaviours

- ❌ Cover photography (the cover is debossed, never imaged).
- ❌ Multiple garments per plate.
- ❌ Photographs on text pages.
- ❌ Ornament: rules, drop caps, decorative borders, footnotes, pull-quotes.
- ❌ Models named or credited beneath their portraits.
- ❌ Letters from the founder, "thanks" pages, or dedications.
- ❌ Sponsorship logos or partner credits on the lookbook itself.
- ❌ A QR code anywhere in the book.
- ❌ A perfect-bound spine. The book opens flat, every page, every time.
- ❌ Glued cover. The cover is sewn to the body block.
- ❌ Reissues, second editions, or "vault" versions.
