# Packaging

Packaging is the customer's first physical encounter with BESOQ. It must feel like the garment inside — restrained, considered, and made for a long life. The system is built around two ideas:

1. **The box is the brand.** The exterior is calm, paper-coloured, near-blank.
2. **The interior is the gift.** The inside reveals one quiet detail — a stamped seal, a printed line, a pattern in the tissue.

---

## The full packaging system

| Tier | Component | Use |
|---|---|---|
| **Outer** | Outer kraft mailer | Shipping, recyclable. |
| **Outer** | Tape — printed | Sealing the mailer. |
| **Inner** | Bone box | Permanent / seasonal garments. |
| **Inner** | Cotton drawstring bag | Knitwear, lightweight pieces. |
| **Inner** | Tissue paper — bone-on-bone weave | Layered inside the box. |
| **Inner** | Care card | Slipped on top of the tissue. |
| **Inner** | Hangtag (delivered separately, attached to garment) | See `02-hangtags-and-labels.md`. |
| **Premium** | Sealing wax + seal | Archive correspondence, repaired pieces returned. |

Every order receives **outer + bone box + tissue + care card** at minimum. Cotton bags replace the box for knit pieces. Sealing wax is reserved for repair returns and ceremonial parcels.

---

## 1. Outer kraft mailer

The brown corrugated mailer is the part of the brand the world sees first. It must look like a parcel — not a marketing surface.

**Material.** Single-wall E-flute corrugated, 100% recycled fibre, FSC-certified.
**Colour.** Natural kraft brown — never bleached, never coloured.
**Size.** Two stock sizes:
- M: 320 × 240 × 80mm (most garments).
- L: 380 × 280 × 120mm (coats, knitwear, two-piece orders).

**Print.** Single screen-printed wordmark `besoq` in **ash ink**, sized at 24mm wide, placed on the **top-left** of the lid. Nothing else on the box. No tagline, no decorative line, no QR code.

**Closure.** Sealed with the printed paper tape (below). No plastic tape. No padded interior. No bubble wrap.

**Inside the mailer.** The bone box (or cotton bag) sits centred, surrounded by a single layer of tissue. There is no void-fill peanuts, no air pillows. If the box does not fill the mailer, a smaller mailer is used.

---

## 2. Paper tape

A printed paper tape used to seal the mailer. Replaces all plastic packing tape.

**Material.** Kraft paper tape, 60mm wide, water-activated.
**Print.** A single repeating line, set in 7pt Söhne Buch:

```
besoq — be so quiet — besoq — be so quiet — besoq — be so quiet —
```

The repeat must be unbroken; the customer should be able to cut the tape anywhere and read a complete fragment.

**Forbidden.** Coloured tape. Patterned tape. Tape with the wordmark in a logo lockup. Tape with a tagline as a single statement.

---

## 3. Inner bone box

The branded box. The quiet centre of the system.

**Material.** Bone-toned uncoated paperboard, 350 gsm, FSC-certified.
**Colour.** Bone (matches `#F4EFE6`).
**Construction.** Two-piece rigid box with a separate lid. No magnets, no ribbon, no insert tray.

**Sizes.**
- S: 240 × 160 × 40mm (shirts, light knits).
- M: 320 × 220 × 60mm (knitwear, trousers).
- L: 400 × 280 × 100mm (coats, jackets).

**Print — exterior.**
- Top-left: wordmark `besoq` debossed at 18mm wide. No ink. The deboss alone carries the mark.
- Bottom-right: tagline `be so quiet.` printed in single-colour ash ink at 6pt.
- Nothing else on the exterior.

**Print — interior (lid underside).**
- Centred on the underside of the lid, a stamped (not printed) seal in **ash ink**, 60mm diameter.
- The seal contains the wordmark `besoq` in its centre.
- This is the brand's "thank you" — quiet, single, hidden until opened.

**Print — interior (base).**
- A single line printed at the base, on one side, 7pt:

> this garment is yours to keep. when it tires, send it home.
> repair@besoq.com

That is all.

---

## 4. Tissue paper

A single sheet of tissue, bone-on-bone weave, wrapping the garment.

**Material.** 22 gsm acid-free tissue paper, bone-toned. FSC-certified.
**Pattern.** The bsq jacquard pattern (see `03-visual/07-illustration-and-pattern.md`) printed in **same-tone bone ink**, visible only at close range. From a distance the tissue reads as plain.
**Sealing.** A single bone-coloured paper sticker, 30mm circle, with the wordmark `besoq` debossed (no ink). Placed at the centre of the folded tissue.

**Forbidden.** Plastic tissue, coloured tissue, scented tissue, branded tape over tissue.

---

## 5. Care card

A single A6 card placed on top of the tissue, before the box is closed.

**Material.** GF Smith Colorplan — Bone, 350 gsm.
**Size.** A6 (105 × 148mm).
**Print method.** Single-colour offset (ash on bone) on the front; letterpress on the back.

**Front.**
```
the long coat — autumn 26
besoq

cloth     380 gsm wool, 100%
mill      [mill name], biella
make      portugal

care
wash dry-clean only
press while damp, low heat
store on a wide hanger
```

**Back.**
A single line, letterpressed:
```
we will repair this. for as long as you wear it.
repair@besoq.com

— besoq
```

Note: the card is per-garment, not per-order. An order with three garments contains three cards.

---

## 6. Cotton drawstring bag (alternative to box)

For knitwear and lighter pieces, a cotton drawstring bag replaces the bone box.

**Material.** 200 gsm undyed organic cotton, GOTS-certified.
**Colour.** Natural / undyed, very close to bone.
**Print.** Wordmark `besoq` screen-printed in ash ink, 28mm wide, centred on the front, 50mm from the top of the bag.
**Closure.** Single drawstring, undyed cotton cord, threaded through a single hem channel. No metal stoppers, no plastic toggles.

**Sizes.**
- S: 280 × 380mm (single shirt).
- M: 360 × 460mm (knitwear).

The bag is **a second life** — designed to be reused as a laundry bag, travel pouch, or drawer organiser. It is not consumable packaging.

---

## 7. Sealing wax & seal (premium / ceremonial)

Reserved for two contexts only:
1. **Archive correspondence** (mail to long-term customers, partners, press).
2. **Returned repaired garments** (a piece sent back after repair carries a seal).

**Wax.** Solid bone-coloured sealing wax (custom blend; provided by a single supplier).
**Seal.** Brass seal, 22mm diameter, engraved with the wordmark `besoq` (matching the seal in `03-visual/01-logo-system.md`).
**Application.** A single wax seal at the centre-top of the envelope or paper sleeve, pressed clean — never streaked.

**Forbidden.** Coloured wax. Imitation wax stickers. Multiple seals on a single surface.

---

## 8. Inserts forbidden

The package never contains:

- ❌ Promotional flyers.
- ❌ Discount cards.
- ❌ "Refer a friend" cards.
- ❌ Sample sachets (perfume, cream, anything).
- ❌ Stickers — branded or otherwise.
- ❌ Influencer-style "thank you" notes from the founder.
- ❌ Anything outside the system above.

Every additional insert is a violation of Pillar 1 (Quiet) and Pillar 5 (Negative space).

---

## 9. Sustainability spec

The packaging system is, by design, low-waste. The full sustainability profile:

| Component | Material | Recycled content | End-of-life |
|---|---|---|---|
| Outer mailer | Recycled corrugated kraft | 100% PCW | Fully recyclable / compostable |
| Paper tape | Recycled kraft | 70%+ | Fully recyclable with the mailer |
| Bone box | Recycled paperboard | 30%+ | Fully recyclable |
| Tissue paper | Acid-free recycled tissue | 60%+ | Fully recyclable / compostable |
| Care card | Colorplan bone | 30%+ | Fully recyclable |
| Cotton bag | GOTS organic cotton | n/a | Reusable indefinitely; biodegradable |
| Sealing wax | Beeswax / shellac blend | n/a | Compostable; remove before recycling |

There is no plastic anywhere in the standard packaging system — including no cellophane, no clear bag, no poly-mailer, no bubble film, no plastic tape, no synthetic ribbon.

---

## 10. Cost philosophy

The packaging is more expensive per unit than industry standard. This is acknowledged — and accepted. The cost is not amortised through cheap inserts, third-party advertising, or upcharges. It is simply built into the price of the garment.

Customers do not need to be told this, but if they ask:

> we use uncoated paper, plant-based ink, and no plastic. the box is more expensive than the alternatives. we built it into the price.

— `care@besoq.com`
