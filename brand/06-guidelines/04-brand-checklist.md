# Brand checklist

A working checklist used before any BESOQ surface ships. The list is intentionally short. A surface that fails any item is held until it passes.

This checklist is the brand owner's last line of defence against drift. It is read every time, even when the team is sure the surface is correct.

---

## A — The strategy check

Before anything else, ask three questions.

- [ ] **A1.** Does this surface trace to at least one of the four pillars? *(Quiet · Considered · Lasting · Honest)*
- [ ] **A2.** Could this surface only have come from BESOQ?
- [ ] **A3.** Is there anything I can cut without losing meaning? *(If yes — cut it before continuing.)*

If A1 fails, the surface is off-strategy. Restart.
If A2 fails, the surface is generic. Restart.
If A3 has a "yes" — return to the surface and remove until A3 is "no."

---

## B — The voice check

If the surface contains words.

- [ ] **B1.** All copy is lowercase (except press releases, legal documents, the editorial wordmark `BESOQ`, and proper nouns).
- [ ] **B2.** Zero exclamation marks.
- [ ] **B3.** Zero emoji.
- [ ] **B4.** No words from the forbidden list in `02-verbal/06-glossary.md` *(iconic, must-have, drop, exclusive, premium, luxury, amazing, etc.)*.
- [ ] **B5.** No urgency mechanics ("hurry," "last chance," "only X left").
- [ ] **B6.** Em dashes (`—`) used, not en dashes or hyphens.
- [ ] **B7.** Curly quotes and curly apostrophes throughout.
- [ ] **B8.** Sign-off uses the canonical form `— besoq` *(when the surface needs a sign-off)*.
- [ ] **B9.** Every sentence either states a fact or gives an instruction. No marketing adjectives.
- [ ] **B10.** Read aloud — does it sound like a maker, not a marketer?

---

## C — The visual check

If the surface contains visual elements.

### C1 — Wordmark
- [ ] Wordmark is at or above the minimum size for its surface (`brand/03-visual/01-logo-system.md`).
- [ ] Wordmark has at least 2x of clear space on all sides.
- [ ] Wordmark is not stretched, skewed, italicised, or stroked.
- [ ] Wordmark is set in Söhne (or Inter as the OSS fallback).
- [ ] Wordmark colour is from the brand palette only.

### C2 — Colour
- [ ] No pure white (`#FFFFFF`) anywhere — bone is the white.
- [ ] No pure black (`#000000`) anywhere — ash is the black.
- [ ] No saturated red/blue/green/yellow.
- [ ] No gradients of any kind.
- [ ] Colour count on this surface is ≤ 3.

### C3 — Type
- [ ] Type is set in one of the three sanctioned families *(Söhne · Times Now · Söhne Mono — or fallbacks)*.
- [ ] Body type is at least 9pt (print) / 14px (web).
- [ ] No bold for emphasis. No italic for emphasis. No underlines except links.
- [ ] No ALL CAPS in body copy.
- [ ] No fully-justified text.
- [ ] No drop caps, ornaments, dingbats.

### C4 — Layout
- [ ] Square corners — no rounded corners anywhere.
- [ ] No drop shadows on any element except a focus ring.
- [ ] Air target met for the surface type *(see `03-visual/04-grid-and-layout.md`)*.
- [ ] Above the fold: no more elements than the density rule allows.
- [ ] No carousels, sticky promo banners, or auto-playing video.

### C5 — Imagery
- [ ] Photography is in one of the four sanctioned modes *(studio garment · on-body neutral · architectural · material study)*.
- [ ] No staged narrative, drone shots, behind-the-scenes, or influencer content.
- [ ] Image carries no watermark.
- [ ] Image alt text describes the content, not the brand.

### C6 — Motion
- [ ] No motion longer than 240ms (except skeleton loops).
- [ ] No parallax, scroll-jacking, or kinetic type.
- [ ] `prefers-reduced-motion` is honoured.

---

## D — The tagline check

If the surface uses a tagline.

- [ ] Only one tagline appears on this surface.
- [ ] The tagline matches the tier appropriate for the surface *(see `02-verbal/03-tagline-system.md`)*.
- [ ] The tagline is not modified, translated, or italicised.
- [ ] The tagline is not used as a button label, hashtag, or CTA.

---

## E — The architecture check

If the surface uses a line, collection, or product name.

- [ ] Format follows `besoq — [line] [year]` exactly.
- [ ] Em dash with single spaces: `besoq — autumn 26` *(not `besoq-autumn-26`, not `BESOQ AW26`)*.
- [ ] Year is two digits, lowercase.
- [ ] Season name is lowercase: `spring`, `autumn` *(never `fall`, `SS25`, `FW26`)*.
- [ ] Garment name preceded by `the` *(never "BESOQ Long Coat")*.

---

## F — The accessibility check

If the surface is digital.

- [ ] Colour contrast meets WCAG AA *(4.5:1 body, 3:1 large)*.
- [ ] All interactive elements have a visible focus ring.
- [ ] All images have descriptive alt text *(or `alt=""` if decorative)*.
- [ ] All form inputs have associated `<label>` elements.
- [ ] Site is fully keyboard-navigable.
- [ ] Reduced-motion preference honoured.

For the full accessibility specification, see `06-guidelines/02-accessibility.md`.

---

## G — The legal check

If the surface is published externally or commercially.

- [ ] The wordmark is used per the trademark guidance in `06-guidelines/03-legal-and-trademark.md`.
- [ ] No third-party fonts, photos, or assets without licence.
- [ ] Mill names, partner names, or collaborator names have been confirmed for public use.
- [ ] No claim of "sustainable," "eco-friendly," "ethical," or "luxury" without specific defensible substantiation.
- [ ] Currency, VAT, and country compliance are correct for the destination market.

---

## H — The final test

Three closing questions before the surface ships.

- [ ] **H1.** Could a luxury house with a quiet posture publish this?
- [ ] **H2.** Could a fast-fashion brand publish this?
- [ ] **H3.** If I received this surface as a customer, would I resent it?

If H1 = yes, H2 = no, H3 = no — ship.
Otherwise, hold and rework.

---

## I — Surface-specific quick checks

Lightweight per-surface refinements of the master checklist.

### Web — homepage
- [ ] One hero image. One line of text. One link.
- [ ] No promotional banner above the nav.
- [ ] No cookie banner that blocks navigation.
- [ ] No carousel.
- [ ] No live chat widget.

### Web — PDP
- [ ] Mill, weight, and country of make are visible without clicking.
- [ ] No reviews, ratings, or "you may also like" carousel.
- [ ] No countdown, no "only X left" indicator.
- [ ] Price has no `.99` ending. Symbol-space-number format.

### Email — marketing
- [ ] Subject line ≤ 6 words, lowercase, no emoji.
- [ ] Body is ≤ 150 words.
- [ ] Single column. Maximum 560px wide.
- [ ] Unsubscribe link is one click.
- [ ] No social-icon row in the footer.

### Social — Instagram
- [ ] Caption ≤ 2 lines (most posts: 1 line).
- [ ] Zero hashtags.
- [ ] Single image. No carousel.
- [ ] Image is 4:5 portrait. No square. No landscape.

### Packaging
- [ ] No plastic anywhere.
- [ ] Wordmark debossed, not printed, on the bone box exterior.
- [ ] No promotional inserts.
- [ ] Care card is present, single A6, paper-stock-correct.

### Hangtag
- [ ] Letterpressed on Colorplan Bone, 350 gsm.
- [ ] Hole-punched (3mm), no eyelet, no plastic seal.
- [ ] Cotton string, not nylon.
- [ ] Wordmark front; tagline back; no QR code.

### Retail — store
- [ ] Wall and floor materials match spec *(bone plaster · oak/concrete)*.
- [ ] Display density ≤ 75% empty space.
- [ ] No music *(or library-only at sub-conversation volume)*.
- [ ] No scent.
- [ ] No window graphics, vinyl signage, or campaign images on the glass.

### Lookbook
- [ ] 64 pages, section-sewn, exposed thread.
- [ ] One garment per plate.
- [ ] No photograph on the cover.
- [ ] Word count ≤ 1,700.

---

## How to use this checklist

1. **Print it.** The checklist works best on paper, not on screen.
2. **Use it every time.** No surface skips. Even a tiny social post runs through B and C.
3. **A "no" is a no.** Ship-blockers are not negotiable.
4. **Update it.** If a surface keeps tripping the same item, that item is teaching us something about the surface; record it in `brand/CHANGELOG.md`.

The checklist is the brand's most boring document. It is also the most important.
