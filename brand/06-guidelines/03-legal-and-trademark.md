# Legal & trademark guidance

This document provides operational guidance on protecting and using the BESOQ name, wordmark, and brand assets. It is **not** legal advice. The brand owner should consult qualified counsel before any registration, enforcement, or licensing decision.

---

| `BESOQ` | Word mark | 25 (apparel), 18 (leather goods), 35 (retail services) |

At launch, primary markets for registration:

- European Union (EUTM via EUIPO).

This is a recommendation, not a registered fact. Confirm with counsel before filing.

## 2. Use of the marks — internal

When BESOQ uses its own marks:

### Wordmark
- Always per `brand/03-visual/01-logo-system.md`.
- Trademark superscript (`™` before registration, `®` after registration in markets where registered) is **suppressed in body copy** for aesthetic reasons. The mark is used only:
  - On the legal page.
  - In legal documents.
  - In trademark notices.
  - On the first prominent use in a press release.

Specifically, the brand's customer-facing surfaces never carry `™` or `®`. The trademark is protected through registration and consistent use, not through symbol-decoration.

### Tagline
- "Be So Quiet" is treated as a slogan trademark.
- Used per the tagline tier system in `02-verbal/03-tagline-system.md`.
- Capitalised when registered as a slogan (legal documents): "Be So Quiet."
- Lowercase in customer copy: "be so quiet."

### Monogram
- Treated as a separate trademark.
- Used only in the contexts allowed by `01-logo-system.md`.

## 3. Use of the marks — by third parties

The default position: **third parties may not use the BESOQ wordmark, monogram, seal, or tagline without written permission.** This applies even when the use is "favourable" to the brand.

### Specific exceptions

| Use case | Allowed? | Conditions |
|---|---|---|
| Editorial press coverage | ✅ | Use of `BESOQ` in body text. Do not modify the wordmark. Wordmark image use requires permission, granted by `press@besoq.com`. |
| Wholesale partner marketing | ✅ | Per signed wholesale agreement. Asset usage limits stated in the contract. |
| Influencer / customer post wearing the brand | ✅ | Tagging `@besoq` is permitted; the wordmark in the customer's photo is permitted; reproducing the wordmark as an overlay is not. |
| Aggregator / marketplace listings | ⚠️ | Permitted only by signed agreement. Resellers without a contract are sent a takedown notice. |
| Affiliate / "drop shipping" use | ❌ | Categorically forbidden. |
| Domain registration containing "besoq" | ❌ | Forbidden. The brand owns its domains and pursues recovery of confusingly-similar registrations. |
| Use in fan art, parody, criticism | ⚠️ | Generally tolerated under fair-use principles. The brand does not enforce against good-faith non-commercial use. |
| Counterfeit goods | ❌ | Categorically forbidden. Pursued through takedowns, customs notifications, and litigation if necessary. |

## 4. Counterfeit & infringement

The brand actively monitors for counterfeit and infringing use. The action ladder:

1. **Detection.** Periodic searches across major marketplaces (eBay, Depop, AliExpress, Vinted, Vestiaire), social platforms, and trademark filings.
2. **Documentation.** Screenshots, listing URLs, seller information, dated and stored.
3. **Cease & desist.** A short, polite letter from counsel or the brand owner. Most issues resolve here.
4. **Platform takedown.** Marketplace IP-infringement reports if the C&D is ignored.
5. **Customs notification.** Recordal with customs in major markets enables seizure of infringing imports.
6. **Litigation.** Reserved for repeat infringement or large-scale counterfeit operations.

The brand does not aggressively pursue de minimis infringement (a fan tee, a small-time copycat shirt). It does pursue counterfeit (a knock-off labelled as BESOQ).

## 5. Domain & social handle protection

The brand defensively registers, in addition to `besoq.com`:

- `besoq.az` (Azerbaijan TLD).
- `besoq.eu`.
- `besoq.co`.
- `besoq.shop`.
- `besoq.store`.
- `besoq.studio`.
- `besoq.world`.
- `besoq.io`.

If a third party registers a confusingly-similar domain (e.g., `besoq-official.com`), the brand pursues recovery via UDRP / URS / national equivalents.

For social handles, `@besoq` on every major platform is registered defensively, even on platforms the brand does not actively use *(LinkedIn, Threads, etc.)*. This prevents impersonation.

## 6. Likeness & model releases

For all photography that includes a recognisable person:

- A **signed model release** is required before the image is published.
- The release must specify:
  - The model's name and contact.
  - The scope of use (web, print, social, all media).
  - The duration (perpetual or term-limited).
  - Whether the model has approval rights over final images (default: no).
  - The compensation paid.
- Releases are stored centrally and indexed by image filename.

For **studio team members** appearing incidentally, a separate, lighter release covers internal photography of work-in-progress.

The brand does not publish photography of people without a release. The brand does not publish photography of customers without explicit permission, even when the customer has shared the photo publicly.

## 7. Mill & maker disclosure

The brand publishes mill and maker names on product pages. Before any disclosure:

- The mill / maker has provided **written permission** to be named publicly.
- The disclosure has been reviewed for accuracy by the studio's sourcing lead.
- The disclosure does not reveal proprietary commercial terms.

Some mills prefer not to be named publicly; they are listed by region only ("a mill in Biella"). The brand respects this preference and does not use ambiguity as a marketing device.

## 8. Sustainability claims

The brand is conservative on sustainability claims. The legal posture:

- **What we may say:** specific, verifiable statements ("plastic-free packaging," "FSC-certified paper," "GOTS-certified cotton in this garment," "lifetime repair").
- **What we may not say without backing:** "sustainable," "eco-friendly," "ethical," "responsible," "carbon-neutral," "climate-positive," "zero-waste."

This is not only a brand position (Pillar 4 — Honest); it is also a legal position. EU Green Claims Directive and FTC Green Guides increasingly penalise unsubstantiated environmental claims. The brand's voice happens to align with the regulatory direction.

If a defensible sustainability claim becomes available (e.g., a verified LCA), it may be used. The brand does not use unsubstantiated greenwashing while waiting for verification.

## 9. Material composition disclosure

Garments are labelled per:

| Market | Standard |
|---|---|
| EU | Regulation (EU) No 1007/2011 — fibre composition labelling. |
| UK | Textile Products (Labelling and Fibre Composition) Regulations 2012. |
| US | 16 CFR Part 303 — Textile Fiber Products Identification Act. |
| Azerbaijan | Local technical regulations on textile labelling. |

The inner composition label carries:
- Fibre composition (in descending order, by weight, in percent).
- Country of make.
- Care symbols (ISO 3758).
- Size (per `04-size-guide.md`).

For markets requiring specific languages, the inner label is multilingual *(EU label rules)*. The brand does not produce English-only labels for mainland European markets.

## 10. Customer data & privacy

| Topic | Position |
|---|---|
| Lawful basis | Contract (orders), legitimate interests (service emails), consent (marketing opt-in). |
| Data minimisation | Collect only what we need to ship and serve. |
| Tracking | None across the open web. No third-party pixels. No GA. |

| Children's data | Site not directed at children under 16. |
| Subject access | Honoured within 30 days. Reply via `care@besoq.com`. |

The privacy policy at `/legal/privacy` is plain English. The brand does not write a 6,000-word privacy policy; it writes the shortest one that is legally complete.

## 11. Repair service — legal note

The "lifetime repair" promise is offered as a service commitment, not a legal warranty. The customer's statutory rights under consumer law are unaffected and superior to anything BESOQ offers commercially.

The brand explicitly states this in the customer-facing copy:

```
this repair service is in addition to your statutory rights as a consumer.
nothing here limits or replaces them.
```

## 12. Press, mention, and quoting

For media use:

- **Press kit** at `besoq.com/press` (when published) contains approved imagery and approved quotes.
- The press kit specifies the exact form in which the wordmark must be used.
- Quotes attributed to the brand owner are approved by the brand owner before publication.
- The brand corrects misquotation politely; it does not pursue litigation for press errors.

## 13. Forbidden legal behaviours

The brand will not:

- ❌ Use a `™` or `®` symbol decoratively in marketing copy.
- ❌ Threaten legal action without counsel involvement.
- ❌ Send aggressive C&Ds to small-time fan use.
- ❌ Pursue unenforceable claims for SEO purposes.
- ❌ Hide jurisdiction in customer-facing terms.
- ❌ Bury an arbitration clause in checkout T&Cs.
