# Care & repair

A BESOQ garment is designed to be worn for years. The brand's care and repair programme is the operational expression of Pillar 3 (Lasting). This document specifies how garments are cared for and repaired — the language, the offer, and the flow.

---

## Philosophy

> a garment is in relationship with its wearer. care is how the relationship is maintained.

The brand's posture on care is corrective to industry standards. We do not push customers to buy more by treating their existing garments as disposable. We treat them as renewable.

---

## 1. Care instructions — the standard

Every BESOQ garment ships with care instructions on three surfaces:

1. **The inner composition label** — ISO 3758 symbols + plain-language line.
2. **The care card** (inside packaging) — full instructions for the specific cloth.
3. **The product page on `besoq.com`** — same instructions, structured.

### Plain-language template

The care card uses a strict three-line plain-language structure:

```
care
[washing instruction]
[drying instruction]
[storage / pressing instruction]
```

### Examples by cloth

#### Cotton (140 GSM poplin, the boxy shirt)

```
care
wash cold, inside out.
line dry.
press while damp.
```

#### Wool (380 GSM pressed, the long coat)

```
care
dry-clean only.
press while damp, low heat.
store on a wide hanger.
```

#### Knitwear (heavy three-stitch)

```
care
hand-wash cool, or dry-clean.
flat dry, away from heat.
fold; do not hang.
```

#### Linen (medium-weight)

```
care
wash cool, inside out.
line dry; embrace the creases.
press while damp if smooth is desired.
```

The brand's voice is direct. Care instructions are written as instructions, not as suggestions.

---

## 2. Symbols

The brand uses **ISO 3758 standard care symbols** — never custom-drawn. The symbols sit at 4mm height on the inner composition label, in single-colour ash on bone tape.

Custom illustration of care symbols would be loud and risk legal compliance. The system uses the standard.

## 3. The repair service

The repair service is the most operationally important non-product programme the brand runs.

### The offer

> we will repair this. for as long as you wear it.

This line appears on:
- The hangtag back.
- The care card back.
- The PDP "repair" section.
- The footer of every customer-care email.
- The studio repair counter.

The offer is **literal and unbounded**. There is no time limit, no purchase-proof requirement, no fee. A garment cut from a different cloth ten years from now is still repaired.

### What is repaired

| Repair type | Always covered |
|---|---|
| Seam re-stitching | Yes |
| Hem re-finishing | Yes |
| Button replacement (with original spec) | Yes |
| Buttonhole reinforcement | Yes |
| Pocket re-attachment | Yes |
| Zipper replacement | Yes |
| Lining repair / replacement | Yes |
| Patch on damaged area | Yes (visible patch acceptable; see below) |
| Re-knitting of small areas | Yes (knitwear) |
| Re-weaving of small holes | Yes, where possible |
| Cleaning of post-repair area | Yes |

### What is not repaired (the honesty)

| Beyond repair | Reason |
|---|---|
| Garments shrunk past 5% of original size | Material change, not damage |
| Garments dyed or bleached after purchase | Material change |
| Garments altered by a third party | Pattern integrity lost |
| Catastrophic damage (fire, large area destroyed) | Beyond practical repair |

When repair is not possible, the brand offers **replacement at cost** — the customer pays only the make cost, not the full retail. This is a quiet generosity, not a marketed benefit.

## 4. Repair flow

### Customer initiates

Customer writes to `repair@besoq.com` with:
- Garment SKU (from inner label or order history).
- A photograph of the area.
- A short description.

### Studio responds (within 3 working days)

A single email from the repair team:

```
ayla,

we received your request. yes — we can repair the long coat.
the cuff replacement takes about three weeks.

please send the garment to:

besoq — repair
[address]

we will pay return shipping.

— samra, besoq repair
```

### Customer ships

The customer can either:
- Bring the garment to a retail location.
- Ship it to the studio (the studio sends a pre-paid label).

### Repair (typical 2–4 weeks)

The garment is repaired in the studio's repair atelier. A small fabric repair sticker is stitched into the inner label (see `04-applications/02-hangtags-and-labels.md`):

> repaired · 03.2027

### Garment returns

The garment is returned in a kraft mailer with:
- A small bone-coloured slip describing the repair.
- A wax seal on the slip (the only standard use of sealing wax in the system).
- A single line: `repaired. we hope it serves another season.`

There is no follow-up email asking for a review.

---

## 5. The "repaired" mark — public-facing

Customers who have had a garment repaired by the studio carry a quiet mark of it inside the garment — the dated repair sticker. This is treated as a feature, not a flaw. It is the visible record of a garment kept.

The brand will, occasionally, photograph these repair marks for the journal. Never with the customer's name, never as marketing, always as a quiet observation.

## 6. Care service — adjacent to repair

For minor adjustments that are not repair (a hem to be shortened, a cuff to be turned), the studio offers **alterations** at cost:

| Alteration | Approximate cost (€, indicative) |
|---|---|
| Shorten trouser hem | 18 |
| Take in waist (1 size) | 24 |
| Shorten sleeve | 22 |
| Re-buttonhole | 12 |
| Replace lining (jacket) | 60 |

These prices are at-cost — the brand does not profit from alterations. The line is included in the repair email if requested.

## 8. Returns policy

Separate from repair, the brand offers **30-day returns** for unworn, undamaged garments. The policy is:

- Returns are free for the customer (the brand pays return shipping).
- A return must be initiated within 30 days of delivery.
- The garment must be unworn, with all tags attached.
- Refund issued within 7 working days of garment receipt.
- No restocking fees.
- No "store credit instead of refund" pressure.

### What the brand does not do
- ❌ "Final sale" tags.
- ❌ Store credit offered as a "thank you" for returning.
- ❌ Charging for return shipping.
- ❌ A "tell us why you're returning" form longer than 3 fields.
- ❌ Photo verification of return condition.

A customer who returns five orders in a row is, in the brand's logic, a customer who has not yet found the right size. The team writes to them, by name, offering a sizing call. There is no penalty.

## 9. Sustainability claims

The brand makes only the sustainability claims it can defend:

- **What we say.** Plastic-free packaging. FSC-certified paper. Repair service. Made in Portugal / Italy. Permanent line restocked rather than redesigned.
- **What we do not say.** "Sustainable." "Eco-friendly." "Conscious." "Responsible." "Ethical" — without specifics.

The brand's position is that **sustainability is operational, not branded**. We do the work without taking the credit, on the principle that brands that market sustainability tend to do less of it.

---

## 10. The annual review

Every January, the brand publishes a short review of the previous year's repairs:

| Metric | Reported as |
|---|---|
| Total garments repaired | A number. |
| Garments per repair (average) | A ratio. |
| Most common repair | A garment + a cause (e.g., "cuff fraying — the long coat"). |
| Garments retired beyond repair | A number. |

This is published in `/journal/` as a single page. It is one of approximately 4–6 journal pages per year.

The annual review exists because **a brand that says "we repair forever" must be able to show its working**. Otherwise the line is marketing.
