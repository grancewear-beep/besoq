# Collection system

The brand's collection model is small, slow, and deliberately repetitive. This document specifies how collections are named, structured, sized, and released.

The system below is enforced. It is not a guideline. A deviation requires an explicit pillar review.

---

## 1. The four lines (recap from architecture)

| Line | What it is | Cadence | SKU count |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Permanent** | Always-on core. Restocked, not redesigned. | Continuous, restocked twice yearly. | ~30 SKUs at maturity |
| **Seasonal** | Spring + Autumn collections. | 2 per year. | ~20 SKUs each |
| **Archive** | Past seasonal stock at original price. | Continuous, until depletion. | Variable |
| **Studies** | Numbered limited explorations. | 2–4 per year, irregular. | 3–6 SKUs each |

See `01-strategy/07-brand-architecture.md` for the strategic context.

---

## 2. Naming convention

### Format

```
besoq — [line] [year]
```

### Examples

| Line | Naming | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Permanent | `besoq — permanent` | No year. The line has no calendar. |
| Spring 2026 | `besoq — spring 26` | Year is two digits, lowercase. |
| Autumn 2026 | `besoq — autumn 26` | Always "autumn." Never "fall." |
| Archive | `besoq — archive` | The line, not the season. |
| Study | `besoq — study no. 03` | Lowercase, "no." abbreviated. |

### Rules
- The em dash is mandatory and uses single spaces around it.
- Year is always two digits (`26`, not `2026` and never `'26`).
- Season names are lowercase: `spring`, `autumn`. Never `SS25`, `FW26`, `S/S 26`.
- Studies are numbered, not named. The number persists permanently — there is only ever one `study no. 03`.
- The brand never uses "collection no. X" naming. The line system is the structure.

### Forbidden naming patterns
- ❌ `BESOQ Spring/Summer 2026 Collection`
- ❌ `BESOQ SS26`
- ❌ `BESOQ The Quiet Collection`
- ❌ `BESOQ × Capsule`
- ❌ `BESOQ Limited Edition`
- ❌ `BESOQ x [self]`
- ❌ Themed collection names (`The Architect`, `The Returner`, `Eclipse`, `Origins`).

The brand does not theme its seasons. A season is identified by year and material — nothing more.

## 3. Garment naming

A garment within a line follows this convention:

```
the [garment]
besoq — [line] [year]
```

### Examples
- `the long coat` — `besoq — autumn 26`
- `the boxy shirt` — `besoq — permanent`
- `the trouser, wide` — `besoq — spring 26`
- `the knit, three-stitch` — `besoq — autumn 26`

### Rules
- Always preceded by `the` — never "BESOQ Long Coat" or "Boxy Shirt by BESOQ".
- Lowercase descriptive name.
- The descriptor is **literal**, not poetic. Not "the silver linings shirt" — just "the boxy shirt."
- A modifier follows after a comma: `the trouser, wide` / `the trouser, slim`.
- The brand has no garment with a person's name (no "the Sevda dress").

### Garment names already established (foundational)
The following are reserved as canonical names — they may evolve in cut but keep their names:

```
the boxy shirt
the placket shirt
the trouser, wide
the trouser, slim
the long coat
the chore coat
the knit, three-stitch
the tee, single-jersey
the henley
the field jacket
```

## 4. Seasonal release calendar

The brand's calendar is annual, predictable, and slow.

| Month | Event |
|---|---|
| **March (week 2)** | Spring collection releases. |
| **April–August** | Permanent line restocks roll through. |
| **September (week 2)** | Autumn collection releases. |
| **October–February** | Permanent line restocks roll through. |
| **December (week 1)** | Spring archive opens (previous spring). |
| **June (week 1)** | Autumn archive opens (previous autumn). |

This calendar is the **only** marketing calendar. The brand does not participate in:

- ❌ Black Friday / Cyber Monday.
- ❌ Boxing Day sales.
- ❌ Lunar New Year capsules.
- ❌ Mother's / Father's / Valentine's Day promotions.
- ❌ Pride-month merchandise.
- ❌ Anniversary sales.
- ❌ Pop-up "warehouse sales."

Every customer-facing reference to time follows this calendar.

## 5. Collection size

| Line | SKU count | Reasoning |
|---|---|---|
| Permanent | ~30 (max 36) | A wardrobe core. Larger than this and the line drifts into trend. |
| Seasonal | ~20 (max 24) | A focused statement. Larger than this and the season blurs. |
| Studies | 3–6 | A study is a single material or silhouette explored. |
| Archive | Variable | Whatever remains from past seasons. |

When a count would exceed the maximum, the answer is to **cut**, not to expand. The cut piece either becomes a permanent candidate or is held for a future season.

## 6. Colour offering

Each garment is offered in a small set of colours — never all eight palette tones.

| Garment type | Colour count | Common pairings |
|---|---|---|
| Tops (shirts, tees) | 2–3 | bone + ash + stone |
| Knitwear | 2–4 | bone + ash + stone + sage (spring) / clay (autumn) |
| Trousers | 2 | ash + stone |
| Outerwear | 1–2 | stone (autumn), bone (spring) |
| Studies | 1 | the single colour of the study |

A garment offered in five or more colours is a marketing decision, not a design one. The brand resists the temptation.

---

## 7. Size offering

The brand offers garments in **continental letter sizes** for tops and outerwear (`XS, S, M, L, XL`) and **numbered sizes** for trousers (`44, 46, 48, 50, 52, 54`).

Knitwear and shirts are typically cut **boxy and unisex** in one size system. Tailored garments — coats, blazers — may carry both letter and numbered sizes if the cut warrants it.

The brand does not use:
- ❌ "One size fits all."
- ❌ "Free size."
- ❌ Designer-house sizing (38–46 for women's).

See `04-size-guide.md` for the full size table.

## 8. Pricing convention

| Line | Price range (€, indicative) | Pricing logic |
|---|---|---|
| Permanent — top | 90–140 | Cost-plus, transparent. |
| Permanent — knit | 180–280 | |
| Permanent — trouser | 160–240 | |
| Seasonal — outerwear | 480–880 | |
| Seasonal — heavy knit | 280–420 | |
| Studies | Material-dependent | Often above seasonal — a study is the brand's most expensive line per piece. |

### Pricing rules
- Prices are listed without `.99` endings. `€ 240`, never `€ 239,99`.
- Currency symbol: `€` for Euro, `$` for USD, `£` for GBP, `₼` for Azerbaijani Manat.
- Currency placement: symbol before the number, with a non-breaking space: `€ 240`.
- Decimal: comma in EU markets, period in US/UK. Tabular figures.
- No "from" prices ("from €240"). The price is the price.

### Price changes
- Permanent line prices do not change within a calendar year, even if input costs rise.
- Permanent line prices may rise once per year, in January, with a single line in the `/journal/` explaining why.
- Seasonal prices are fixed at release and do not change during the season — including not declining as the season ends.
- **Archive does not discount.** This is non-negotiable.

## 9. Discounting policy

The brand's policy on discounting is simple and absolute:

> **the brand does not discount.**

There are no:
- ❌ Site-wide sales.
- ❌ Flash sales.
- ❌ Coupon codes.
- ❌ "First-order 10% off."
- ❌ Newsletter-signup discounts.
- ❌ Loyalty-tier discounts.
- ❌ Influencer discount codes.
- ❌ "Friends and family" 30% off events.
- ❌ "We're sorry the site was slow, here's a code" apologies.

The only price reduction in the brand's existence is for **defective garments** — handled as a refund or replacement, not a coupon.

This policy is the largest single marketing decision the brand makes. It is also the most defensible. Once a brand discounts, the customer waits for the next discount.

## 10. The release ritual

Each seasonal release follows the same ritual — uneventfully on purpose.

| T-7 days | A single postcard mailed to long-term customers. One line on the front: `[collection]. [date].` |
| T-3 days | The collection page is staged at a private URL; press receives the lookbook. |
| T-1 day | The collection page becomes accessible at `/spring-26` etc., with a "releases tomorrow" line. |
| T-0 (release day) | The collection becomes available, at 10:00 local time of the studio. A single email goes out. A single Instagram post. That is all. |
| T+1 to T+14 | No further marketing. The work is done. |

The release is not "celebrated." There is no live-stream, no launch event, no countdown.

---

## 11. The closing ritual

Each seasonal collection ends as quietly as it begins.

- The collection page remains live for 90 days after release.
- After 90 days, remaining stock moves to `/archive` at unchanged prices.
- The original collection page redirects to the archive page.
- A single line of editorial copy remains on the archive page: `from the [season] [year] collection.`
- No "FINAL CALL — last chance to buy" emails. None.

---

## 12. Permanent line ritual

The permanent line is the brand's spine. Its ritual is invisible:

- Restocked twice per year (April + October).
- A single email sent to "notify me" subscribers per restock.
- No public "the boxy shirt is back!" announcement.
- The garment simply returns — like a door opening to a room that was always there.

---

## 13. Forbidden product behaviours

- ❌ Naming a collection after a feeling, a place, or a season-mood.
- ❌ Cross-collection capsules ("BESOQ × Tokyo," "BESOQ Holiday").
- ❌ Limited editions framed as scarcity marketing.
- ❌ Pre-orders (we ship from stock — see `06-materials-library.md` for cloth holds).
- ❌ Variable pricing by region beyond duty/VAT differences.
- ❌ Discount stacking, holiday codes, abandoned-cart percent-off offers.
- ❌ "Drop" launches at midnight or any other manufactured time.
- ❌ Restocking a permanent piece in a "new colour" that is the trend colour of the year.
