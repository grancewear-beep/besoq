# Photography

Photography is the most expensive surface BESOQ produces. It also drifts the fastest. This document is the canonical reference for every image the brand commissions, approves, or publishes.

---

## Photographic philosophy

> photograph the garment as you would photograph a person you respect — once, fully, and without interrupting them.

BESOQ's photography is **observational**, not staged. The camera records a garment that is already there; it does not perform the garment into being.

---

## The four photographic modes

The brand uses four — and only four — photographic modes. Every image must clearly belong to one of them.

### Mode 1 — Studio garment (still life)

Garment laid or hung against a single neutral wall, photographed in soft daylight or single-source studio light. No model. No styling props.

**Use.** PDP primary image. Lookbook plate. Email banner.

**Specifications.**
- Background: Bone, Linen, or Stone wall.
- Lens: 50mm or 85mm equivalent.
- Aperture: f/4 to f/8 — sharp throughout.
- Light: 1 large source, 4500–5500K.
- Frame: garment fills 60% of vertical, centred or offset to right third.

### Mode 2 — On-body, neutral

Model wears the garment against a single neutral wall. No direction (no laughing, no leaning), no movement, no narrative.

**Use.** PDP secondary image. Editorial campaigns.

**Specifications.**
- Pose: stand, hand at side, eyes level or slightly down.
- Crop: full body or three-quarter — never cropped above the waist for a primary.
- Background: bone, linen, or in some cases an architectural wall.
- Light: window light preferred. If artificial, 4500–5500K with a single soft source.
- Expression: neutral. Slight, contained presence. No smiles, no smirks, no posed melancholy.

### Mode 3 — Architectural

Garment in an architectural setting — a stone courtyard, a concrete corridor, a cloister, a gallery. The architecture and the garment share the frame; the model is incidental to both.

**Use.** Lookbook spreads. Seasonal campaign hero.

**Specifications.**
- Architecture must be **quiet**: minimal ornament, monochrome materials.
- Lens: 35mm equivalent. Wide enough to read the space.
- Light: ambient daylight, no fill.
- Composition: garment occupies one third of the frame; architecture two thirds.

### Mode 4 — Material study

Close-up of the cloth itself. No model, no garment shape — only weave, weight, and texture.

**Use.** Material story pages. PDP supplementary tile. Journal.

**Specifications.**
- Lens: macro or 85mm with extension.
- Light: raking side-light to reveal weave structure.
- Frame: cropped tight on a single texture; no garment edges visible.

---

## Forbidden photographic modes

These are explicitly off-brand and must never be commissioned or published.

- ❌ **Lifestyle / staged narrative.** No "couple laughing on a beach," no "girls on a bench in golden hour."
- ❌ **Drone, aerial, or distance hero shots.** The brand is intimate. The drone is not.
- ❌ **Backstage / behind-the-scenes.** Reveals the artifice. The brand prefers stillness over process.
- ❌ **Influencer or self-portrait photography.** No selfies, no mirror shots, no front-camera looks.
- ❌ **Heavy retouching.** Skin imperfections, fabric texture, and natural shadows are kept.
- ❌ **Monogram-emphasising crops.** No close-up on a label. The garment is photographed as a garment, not as a logo.

---

## Light

| Specification | Required |
|---|---|
| Colour temperature | 4500–5500K (daylight) |
| Direction | Single source, soft, side or front |
| Contrast | Medium-low; no deep shadows |
| Reflectors / fill | Minimal; one bounce card maximum |
| Flash | Forbidden in primary photography |
| Strobe | Forbidden in primary photography |

The brand prefers **window light** wherever possible. When it is not, a single large softbox at 5500K replicates window light at scale.

---

## Colour grading

Photography is graded toward **warm neutral**. Specifically:

- Whites pulled to bone (slightly yellow-warm).
- Blacks softened to ash (never crushed to pure black).
- Saturation reduced 10–15% from camera default.
- Vibrance untouched.
- Skin tones held warm, never desaturated to greenish or pulled to magenta.

A grading LUT for the system lives in `brand/assets/photography/besoq-grade-v1.cube` (placeholder — to be added by the photographic lead in production).

## Casting

BESOQ casts to a quiet posture, not a demographic.

**What we look for:**
- A presence that does not perform for the camera.
- A posture that holds without direction.
- An age range that reflects the wardrobe — primarily 28 to 55.
- A range of body types that the garment serves; not just a single industry-standard fit.

**What we do not cast:**
- Influencers with established personal brands.
- Models below 22.
- Anyone who has been the face of a competitor in the past 12 months.

---

## Composition rules

1. **Subject left or right; never centred.** Centred composition is loud. Off-centre is quiet.
2. **Negative space dominates.** At least 50% of every frame is empty.
3. **Faces, when shown, look down or away.** Direct eye contact is rare and intentional.
4. **No rule of thirds gimmickry.** The grid is internal; do not enforce it visually.
5. **One garment per frame.** Layered styling is acceptable, but the photograph is of one garment, not five.

## Resolution & file standards

| Surface | Resolution | File format |
|---|---|---|
| PDP primary | 2400 × 3000 (4:5) | JPEG (q 88) + AVIF |
| PDP secondary | 2000 × 2500 (4:5) | JPEG (q 88) + AVIF |
| Lookbook print | 300 dpi at 1:1 print size | TIFF (CMYK) |
| Social — feed | 1080 × 1350 (4:5) | JPEG (q 90) |
| Social — story | 1080 × 1920 (9:16) | JPEG (q 90) |
| Email | 1200 × 1500 (4:5) | JPEG (q 85) |
| Wholesale press kit | 4000 × 5000 | TIFF (RGB) + JPEG |

All images carry **no watermark**. The brand does not stamp its name on its own photography. If an image is reused without credit, that is a problem to solve with an attribution culture, not with a watermark.

---

## File naming

Photography files follow this naming convention:

```
besoq_{collection}_{garment-slug}_{mode}_{model-or-still}_{frame}.{ext}
```

Examples:
- `besoq_aw26_long-coat_studio_still_01.jpg`
- `besoq_aw26_long-coat_onbody_neutral-01_03.jpg`
- `besoq_permanent_boxy-shirt_material_macro_01.tif`

This naming convention is non-negotiable. It is what allows the team to find an image six seasons from now.

---

## Approval flow

Every image, before it is published, is checked against a single test:

> **could this image only have come from BESOQ?**

If the answer is yes — the framing, the light, the casting, the cloth, the calm — the image is approved.

If the image could just as easily be from COS, Everlane, Aritzia, Massimo Dutti, or any other contemporary minimalist brand, it is rejected and re-shot.

The test is strict on purpose. Photography is the easiest place for the brand to drift loud.
