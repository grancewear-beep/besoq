# Print specifications

The technical specifications for every printed surface BESOQ produces. This document is the reference handed to print partners — paper houses, foilers, embossers, finishers — and to any internal team commissioning a printed asset.

---

## Paper philosophy

> the paper is part of the message. coated paper says "marketing." uncoated paper says "made."

BESOQ uses uncoated, warm-toned paper for almost every printed surface. There are no glossy brochures, no coated postcards, no laminated business cards.

| Stock | Colour | Weight | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Arena Natural Smooth** | Bone-warm | 120 gsm | Letterhead, envelopes, care cards. |
| **Arena Natural Smooth** | Bone-warm | 300 gsm | Business cards, hangtags, postcards. |
| **GF Smith Colorplan — Bone** | Bone | 350 gsm | Premium hangtags, presentation cards. |
| **Materica — Limestone** | Stone | 250 gsm | Invitations, archive cards. |
| **Old Mill Premium White** | Linen | 250 gsm | Lookbook covers (uncoated finish). |
| **Munken Pure** | Bone-warm | 100 gsm | Lookbook interior (text pages). |

All stocks are FSC-certified at minimum. PCW recycled stock is preferred where available.

### Stocks explicitly forbidden
- ❌ Any coated paper (gloss, silk, or matte-coated).
- ❌ Bleached pure-white stocks.
- ❌ Plastic-laminated paper of any kind.
- ❌ Iridescent, metallic, or pearlescent stocks.

## Print methods

### Default — single-colour offset
The brand's default print method. One colour (Ash on Bone) on uncoated stock. Most BESOQ printed surfaces are produced this way.

### Letterpress
Used for premium pieces — business cards, invitations, archive cards. Letterpress on Colorplan or Materica is the brand's "ceremonial" treatment.

### Foil — matte only
Foil is permitted only in **matte white** or **matte black** on bone or ash stocks respectively. Never gloss. Never gold, silver, or coloured foil.

### Embossing & debossing
The brand's preferred decorative finish. The wordmark is debossed (recessed) on most premium surfaces — boxes, hangtags, cards. The deboss is shallow: 0.3mm.

### Forbidden print methods
- ❌ Spot UV.
- ❌ Holographic foiling.
- ❌ Soft-touch lamination.
- ❌ Edge gilding.
- ❌ Die-cut decorative shapes (functional die-cuts are permitted).
- ❌ Thermography (raised gloss type).

## Inks

For four-colour offset (rare, for photography-driven lookbook interiors only):

| Ink | Use |
|---|---|
| Standard CMYK | Lookbook image pages |
| Pantone 9224 U | Bone, where the paper itself isn't bone-toned |
| Pantone Black 6 U | Ash, single-colour |
| Pantone 7530 U | Stone |
| Pantone 5777 U | Sage |

The brand prefers **single-colour offset** in Pantone Black 6 U on uncoated bone-toned paper for the vast majority of print work. Four-colour is the exception.

## Trim & finish

| Element | Spec |
|---|---|
| Trimming | Single-knife, square-edge. |
| Edge | Natural — no painted, gilded, or coloured edges. |
| Folds | Crease-and-fold for stocks above 200 gsm. |
| Binding (lookbook) | Section-sewn, exposed spine; no glue-only perfect binding. |
| Corners | Square only. No rounded corners. |

## Garment label specifications

### Outer (visible) label — woven
- Fabric: woven cotton tape.
- Colour: bone tape with ash text (or ash tape with bone text on dark garments).
- Size: 30 × 12mm.
- Type: `besoq` only. No size, no composition.
- Placement: centre-back, 4mm below collar seam.

### Inner (composition) label — printed
- Fabric: smooth poly-cotton blend, 25 × 80mm.
- Print: single-colour ash on bone tape.
- Information order:
  1. `besoq`
  2. Composition (`100% cotton`)
  3. Country of make (`made in portugal`)
  4. Size (`size m`)
  5. Care symbols (ISO 3758 standard)
  6. Tagline: `be so quiet.` (bottom)
- Placement: side seam, left side of garment, 12mm below the side-seam pocket entry (or the equivalent reference point for the garment type).

## Production checklist

Before any printed surface is signed off for production, it must pass this checklist:

- [ ] Stock is on the approved list.
- [ ] Print method is on the approved list.
- [ ] Ink is on the approved list.
- [ ] No coated paper.
- [ ] No rounded corners.
- [ ] No drop shadow, gradient, or stroke on type.
- [ ] Wordmark falls within minimum and maximum size rules.
- [ ] Body type is at least 9pt.
- [ ] No exclamation marks in any copy.
- [ ] Bleed and safe area are correct.
- [ ] An ICC profile (FOGRA52 for uncoated, FOGRA51 for coated if rare exception) is embedded.

If a single item fails, the surface is held until it passes.
