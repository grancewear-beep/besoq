# Brand principles — the long form

These principles are non-negotiable. Every decision — from a fabric purchase to an Instagram caption — is checked against them. When two principles conflict, the one earlier in the list wins.

---

## 1. Whisper, don't shout.

Restraint is the brand's competitive moat. Anyone can shout; very few can stay quiet under pressure. A loud garment ages in a season; a quiet one ages with the wearer. When in doubt, remove an element rather than add one.

**Operational implications**
- No prints larger than a postage stamp.
- No fluorescent or saturated primary colours.
- No exclamation marks in any owned copy, ever.
- No drop countdowns, no "hurry, only 3 left" mechanics.

## 2. Cloth before logo.

The material is the marketing. We invest in mills, weights, and hands before we invest in advertising. Customers should be able to recognise BESOQ blindfolded by touch alone within five seasons.

**Operational implications**
- Mill and weight are disclosed on every product page.
- Fabric composition is featured before styling on the PDP.
- Hangtags display weight in GSM, not vague adjectives.

## 3. Proportion is identity.

We do not own a print or a logo. We own a silhouette. The BESOQ shoulder line, sleeve pitch, and trouser break should be recognisable without any label visible.

**Operational implications**
- Pattern blocks are versioned and protected in `product/blocks/`.
- A silhouette that does not pass the "back-turned test" (recognisable from behind) is rejected.
- New categories must inherit the proportion DNA before they ship.

## 4. Slow on purpose.

We release when a garment is ready, not when a calendar says so. Two collections per year — spring and autumn — plus an always-on permanent line. No pre-fall, no resort, no capsules dressed up as collections.

**Operational implications**
- A delayed launch is preferred to a compromised one.
- Permanent line is restocked, not "re-released."
- Marketing calendar follows production, never the other way around.

## 5. Negative space is design.

White space, silence, and absence are tools as legitimate as type and colour. A page with one image is stronger than a page with six. A campaign with one shot is stronger than a carousel.

**Operational implications**
- Default layout grid leaves 60%+ of the canvas empty.
- Email campaigns: one image, one line, one link.
- Homepage: never more than three editorial blocks above the fold.

## Tension log — known internal conflicts

| Tension | Resolution rule |
|---|---|
| Quiet vs. discoverability (SEO) | Quiet wins on owned surfaces. SEO copy lives in metadata, not on the page. |
| Slow vs. growth pressure | Slow wins for collections. Growth is found in deepening the permanent line. |
| Restraint vs. seasonal storytelling | Restraint wins. Stories are told in the garment, not around it. |
| Direct voice vs. politeness in CS | Direct voice for marketing; warm, full sentences for customer service. |
