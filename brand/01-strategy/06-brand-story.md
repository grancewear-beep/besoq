# Brand story

The brand story is the version of BESOQ told to journalists, partners, retail buyers, and customers who ask. It exists in three lengths. All three say the same thing; none of them embellishes.

---

## 25 words

> BESOQ is a clothing brand built on restraint. The name stands for "Be So Quiet." We make wardrobes that age well and announce nothing.

---

## 100 words

> BESOQ is a contemporary wardrobe brand whose name is an acronym of its philosophy: be so quiet. Founded in response to a decade of logo fatigue, the brand makes garments that earn their place through cloth, cut, and proportion rather than visible branding. Two collections a year are joined by a permanent line of pieces designed to be re-bought, repaired, and kept. Every garment is documented down to the mill and the weight. There are no flash sales, no seasonal capsules, and no exclamation marks. The garment is the message; the message is quiet.

---

## 350 words

> BESOQ began with a question: what would a clothing brand look like if it refused to compete on volume?
>
> The decade leading up to its founding had taught a generation of customers that loud sells faster than quiet — that monograms, drops, and manufactured urgency were not edges of the industry but its centre. By the early 2020s, the centre had begun to feel exhausted, and a quieter customer had emerged: someone who had owned the logo bag, the hyped sneaker, the seasonal capsule — and had given them away.
>
> BESOQ exists for that customer.
>
> The name is an acronym of its position: **Be So Quiet.** It is also a discipline. The wordmark is set small. The seal is hidden inside. The marketing is restrained. The clothes are designed to enter any room without announcing themselves.
>
> The brand operates on five principles: whisper, don't shout; cloth before logo; proportion is identity; slow on purpose; negative space is design. These are not slogans. They are the rules every decision is checked against — from the shoulder line of a coat to the spacing of an Instagram caption.
>
> Two collections are released each year — spring and autumn — joined by a permanent line of pieces that are restocked rather than redesigned. Every garment carries the mill name, the fabric weight in GSM, and the country of make. Every garment is offered with a repair service for as long as it is worn. There are no flash sales. There are no influencer codes. There is no founder myth.
>
> What there is, instead, is the garment.
>
> The brand is independent, contemporary, and direct. It is priced below the great quiet-luxury houses and above the accessible minimalists, in a space the post-logo customer has been quietly waiting for someone to fill.
>
> BESOQ is that someone.
>
> Be so quiet.

## How to use the story

- **Press inquiries** → use the 100-word version as the lede; offer the 350 on request.
- **Wholesale buyers** → lead with the 25 to qualify interest; follow with the 350 if they lean in.
- **About page** → use the 350 as the spine, broken into three sections matching natural breaks.
- **Founder bio** → do not write one yet. The brand should establish itself before the founder does. (See `00-overview/principles.md` — pillar 1.)

## What the story never includes

- A "we started this in our kitchen" anecdote.
- A claim of being "the first" anything.
- The word "passion."
- The word "iconic."
- The word "luxury" without a qualifier we can defend.
- A photograph of the founder for at least the first 18 months.
